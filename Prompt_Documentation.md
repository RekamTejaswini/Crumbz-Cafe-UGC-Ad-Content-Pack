Crumbz Cafe – Prompt Engineering Documentation

1. Introduction

Prompt engineering is the process of providing clear instructions and context to an AI tool to generate useful and relevant outputs.

In this project, ChatGPT was used to develop UGC advertising content for Crumbz Cafe.

2. AI Tool Used

ChatGPT: Used for generating advertisement hooks, video scripts, social media captions and marketing strategies.

3. Prompts Used

Prompt 1 – Advertisement Hooks

Prompt:

Act as a professional UGC advertising copywriter. Generate 10 engaging advertisement hooks for Crumbz Cafe in Chandapura, Bengaluru. Focus on food, beverages, cafe atmosphere and social gatherings.

Purpose: To generate attention-grabbing opening lines.

Prompt 2 – Video Advertisement Scripts

Prompt:

Create three short UGC-style video advertisement scripts for Crumbz Cafe. Each script should include a hook, problem, solution, scene descriptions, dialogue and call to action.

Purpose: To develop short promotional video concepts.

Prompt 3 – Social Media Captions

Prompt:

Generate separate promotional captions for Instagram, Facebook and YouTube Shorts for Crumbz Cafe. Include engaging opening lines, calls to action and relevant hashtags.

Purpose: To create platform-specific promotional content.

Prompt 4 – Marketing Strategy

Prompt:

Develop a UGC content marketing strategy for Crumbz Cafe. Include campaign objectives, target audience, content themes and recommended social media platforms.

Purpose: To organize the advertising campaign.

4. Prompt Engineering Techniques

Role-based prompting: Assigning ChatGPT a professional copywriter role.

Context-based prompting: Providing the cafe name, location and campaign details.

Instruction-based prompting: Clearly specifying the required output.

Structured prompting: Organizing responses into sections and scenes.

Platform-specific prompting: Adapting content for different social media platforms.

5. AI Output Refinement

The generated content was reviewed and organized to improve readability, consistency, audience relevance and promotional appeal.

6. Benefits of Prompt Engineering

Saves time during content ideation.

Generates multiple creative approaches.

Supports platform-specific content creation.

Helps organize marketing campaigns.

Improves the efficiency of content development.

7. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how prompt engineering can be applied to create structured UGC advertising content for a local business using AI tools such as ChatGPT.
