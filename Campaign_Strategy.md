Crumbz Cafe – UGC Content Marketing Strategy

1. Campaign Overview

The campaign aims to promote Crumbz Cafe in Chandapura, Bengaluru, through AI-generated UGC-style advertisements and social media content.

2. Campaign Objectives

Increase local brand awareness.

Attract college students and young professionals.

Promote cafe experiences, food and beverages.

Improve social media engagement.

Encourage potential customers to visit the cafe.

3. Target Audience

College students.

Young professionals.

Friends and social groups.

Food and cafe enthusiasts.

4. Content Strategy

Content Theme 1 – Cafe Experience

Showcase the cafe atmosphere and encourage people to discover the cafe.

Content Theme 2 – Food Promotion

Use appealing food and beverage visuals to attract food lovers.

Content Theme 3 – Friends and Hangouts

Promote the idea of spending quality time with friends at a cafe.

Content Theme 4 – Relatable UGC

Use conversational hooks and everyday situations to connect with viewers.

5. Social Media Platforms

Instagram Reels: Short, engaging promotional videos.

Facebook: Local audience engagement and promotional posts.

YouTube Shorts: Short-form video advertisements for wider content discovery.

6. Suggested Posting Schedule

Monday – Cafe experience video.

Wednesday – Food promotion creative.

Friday – Friends and hangout advertisement.

Sunday – Promotional post or short video.

This is a proposed schedule, not a record of published posts.

7. Audience Engagement Strategy

Use engaging opening hooks.

Include relevant hashtags.

Encourage viewers to tag their friends.

Use clear calls to action.

Respond to genuine audience comments and enquiries.

8. Campaign Performance Measurement

Track the following indicators:

Video views.

Likes and comments.

Shares and saves.

Profile visits.

Customer enquiries.

Click-through rate.

9. Expected Outcomes

Better local brand visibility.

Increased audience engagement.

Improved promotional content quality.

Greater awareness of Crumbz Cafe.

Potential increase in cafe visits.

These are expected outcomes and have not been measured.

10. Conclusion

The proposed UGC marketing strategy combines AI-generated creative content, prompt engineering and social media promotion to support Crumbz Cafe's digital marketing efforts.
